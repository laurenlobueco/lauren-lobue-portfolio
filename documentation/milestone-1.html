Personal Website Project for Lauren LoBue

Purpose, Audience & Goal

Purpose

The project will be a professional portfolio website showcasing my experience in brand development, marketing strategy, go-to-market strategy, and entrepreneurship.

Audience

This site is designed for a professional and entrepreneurial audience and is primarily targeted towards early-stage founders and small business owners who are developing a new business, product, or brand. The secondary audience includes marketing professionals, startup organizations, and potential collaborators interested in brand strategy, marketing and entrepreneurship.

Goal

The primary goal of this site is for early-stage founders to understand how I can help them turn a business idea into a clearly positioned brand with an actionable marketing and go-to-market strategy, and ultimately schedule a consultation to discuss working together. The secondary goal is to showcase my experience, past work, and approach to building brands and bringing products to market.

Persona

Name:Emily Garcia

Age:36

Emily is the founder of an early-stage consumer products company preparing to bring its first product to market. She has spent the past year developing and validating the product and has invested her own money into the business. Emily understands the problem her product solves and has received positive feedback from potential customers, but she has not yet developed a cohesive brand or marketing strategy. She is preparing for launch and needs to determine how to position the company, communicate its value, reach the right customers, and build initial market traction. Essentially, she has been so focused on product development that she needs help organizing all the additional work to get it to market.

Attitudes and Behaviors

Emily is ambitious, creative, resourceful, and highly involved in every part of her business. She has limited time and resources and is accustomed to figuring things out herself. She has researched branding and marketing extensively. She has experimented with AI tools, templates, and online resources, but the amount of information available has made it difficult to determine what actually applies to her business. Emily does not want a large agency or a generic marketing package. She values working directly with someone who understands entrepreneurship and help her make strategic decisions before she spends significant money executing the wrong ideas.

Frustrations and Needs

Emily has a strong product and a clear vision for the company but struggles to turn that vision into a compelling brand story that customers understand and connect with. She is unsure how to position the product against competitors, define her ideal customer, establish the brand's personality and voice, or communicate why the brand matters. She has already invested money in things like a logo, website, social media, and some ads, but those efforts have felt disconnected and have not produced the results she expected. She is frustrated with spending money on marketing without a clear strategy behind it. Emily needs a clear foundation and direction before investing more money into bringing her brand to market.

Goals

Emily wants to confidently launch her company with a brand and marketing strategy that clearly communicates what the business offers, who it serves, and why customers should care. She has also invested her own money and that of friends and family. She is looking for somebody who has experience across all the channels in marketing so she can allocate her money in the most efficient way possible. When visiting Lauren's website, Emily wants to quickly determine whether Lauren understands the challenges of building an early-stage company and whether she can help develop the strategic foundation for the brand. Emily wants to see Lauren's experience, understand how she approaches brand and marketing strategy, and can easily schedule a consultation to discuss working together.

User Story

As an early-stage founder, I need help building my brand and bringing it to market.
